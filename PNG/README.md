India Oil, Rupee & Inflation Impact Analysis

An economic data analysis project using PostgreSQL and Power BI to explore the relationship between crude oil prices, USD-INR exchange rates, inflation, and Indian stock market trends. The interactive dashboard highlights key economic indicators, trends, and potential impacts to support data-driven insights.

**Tools:** PostgreSQL, Power BI  
**Skills:** SQL analysis, data visualization, KPI tracking, trend analysis, and business insights.
